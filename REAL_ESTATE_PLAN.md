# House-Hack Blueprint: FHA Multi-Unit Purchase Plan (San Antonio, TX)

*Prepared September 2026. Planning model only — verify every figure with a licensed mortgage loan officer, a CPA, and the Bexar County Appraisal District before making an offer.*

## The verdict, up front

Goal: buy a 2–4 unit property north of Hwy 90 and inside Loop 1604, live in one unit, rent the rest, using FHA 3.5%-down financing, while preserving spending power and maximizing monthly cash flow to reinvest.

**Recommendation: a duplex, not a triplex or quadplex.** At current San Antonio prices, rent hasn't risen fast enough relative to purchase price for a typical 3–4 unit property to pass FHA's self-sufficiency test, and even when it does, the required 3-month PITI reserve pushes total cash-to-close to $35,000–$60,000+ — well past current savings. A duplex has no self-sufficiency test and no FHA reserve requirement, and is achievable for roughly $19,000–$26,000 cash to close.

**The one exception worth hunting for:** a triplex priced near $95–100k/unit with combined rent near $1,300/unit clears every FHA test and would cover ~95% of the payment with tenant rent. It's a below-average deal you'd have to find — treat it as a stretch goal, not a plan to count on.

## How the FHA math works

1. **3.5% down, 1.75% upfront MIP.** A 740+ credit score qualifies for FHA's minimum down payment. Upfront MIP (1.75% of loan) is typically financed into the loan. Annual MIP runs ~0.55%/year of the loan balance at this down payment level, for the life of the loan.
2. **DTI ceiling: 43% standard, up to ~50% with compensating factors** (740+ credit, cash reserves).
3. **Rental income add-back:** for any 2–4 unit owner-occupied property, a lender can count 75% of market rent from the unit(s) you don't occupy as extra qualifying income.
4. **Self-sufficiency test — 3 & 4 unit properties only:** 75% of market rent for *every* unit, including the one you occupy, must alone cover full PITI + MIP. Pass/fail; personal income cannot fix a property that fails it. Duplexes are exempt.

## Where to look: Tier 1 vs. Tier 2

All neighborhoods below sit north of Hwy 90 and inside Loop 1604.

**Tier 1 (premium, strongest rent):**
- **Tobin Hill** — median multi-family ~$438k; walkable to the Pearl, San Antonio College, downtown; best renter demand, strongest appreciation.

- **Dignowity Hill** — median multi-family ~$325k (range $115k–$1.3M reflects condition spread); gentrifying near-east-side; where the triplex stretch deal math is most plausible.

## Scaling to property #2

FHA requires occupying the property as your primary residence, typically for at least 12 months. After that, you can move out, convert unit 1 fully to a rental, and buy your next primary residence with another 3.5%-down FHA loan — using standard rental-income rules on the new property, plus the now fully-rented income from property #1 as qualifying income. This is the standard way small investors scale on FHA without 20–25% down each time.
